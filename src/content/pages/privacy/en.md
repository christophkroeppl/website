This website sets no cookies for analytics, marketing or statistics and uses no tracking tools. Only data that is technically necessary to operate the site and answer enquiries is processed.

## Controller

Christoph Kröppl. Name, postal address and email address are listed in the [legal notice](/en/contact/).

## Hosting

This website runs as a Cloudflare Worker at Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When the site is accessed, Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time of access, requested resource and user agent in server logs. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in secure and stable operation under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. A data processing agreement is in place with Cloudflare; transfers to the United States rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## Contact form and Turnstile

To prevent automated submissions, the contact form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile processes client signals such as IP address, TLS fingerprint, user agent, and the sitekey and origin used. Turnstile sets no cookies on this website; pre-clearance is not enabled. Cloudflare acts as a processor for bot detection and as an independent controller when improving its own detection methods. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in preventing abuse under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## Delivery of contact requests

The details entered in the form (name, email address, phone number, message) are transmitted via the Telegram Bot API to Telegram Messenger Inc. and stored there so the enquiry can be handled. The legal basis is the performance of your request under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and the legitimate interest in reliable delivery under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Telegram carries out its own data processing for delivery; Telegram's privacy policy applies additionally. Enquiries are deleted once they have been fully handled and no statutory retention obligations apply.

## Contact details on this website

The postal address, email address and phone number are not shipped with the page source. They are requested from the server only after a click and rendered as graphics. That request is not stored, sets no cookies and involves no third parties.
